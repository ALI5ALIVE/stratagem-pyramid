## The problem (verified in code)

The Week Field Kit PDF is generated by `src/lib/fieldKitPdf.ts → renderSlidePagePortrait`. Even though `src/data/salesEnablementStudyNotes.ts` already contains rich curated study notes (`inOneSentence`, `whyItMatters`, `keyIdeas`, `terms`, `facts`, `watchOut`, `connectsTo`, `checkYourself`), the PDF actively throws most of it away:

- `buildStudyNote` does `{ ...base, ...derived }` — whenever a narration script exists (which is every slide), the auto-parsed `narrationInOneLine` + `keyPoints` overwrite/shadow the curated content.
- The renderer then displays `narrationInOneLine` + a numbered list of `keyPoints` (paraphrased narration sentences) as the entire body. `whyItMatters`, `keyIdeas`, `checkYourself` are never drawn.
- There is no block telling the rep "this is what you're looking at on the slide" — so the page reads like coach-talk fragments, not a study sheet.

The user is right: a rep reading the PDF cold cannot reconstruct the slide. They are effectively reading shorthand of the narration audio.

## What the 1-pager should be (rep perspective)

Thinking as a Comply365 sales rep: after reading one page I should be able to walk into a customer meeting and (a) describe what the slide visually shows, (b) deliver the core idea in one sentence, (c) defend it with 2–3 specific facts, (d) handle the obvious objection, and (e) know what comes next in the deck. The page must therefore stand alone — narration becomes optional reinforcement, not the source.

## Changes (technical, scoped to the PDF + its data)

**1. `src/data/salesEnablementStudyNotes.ts`**
- Add `whatsOnSlide: string[]` (3–5 short bullets describing the visual: what shapes/diagram/columns/numbers are on the screen).
- Curate `whatsOnSlide` for every slide that already has a STUDY_NOTES entry (≈50 slides). Use the slide component (e.g. `SEDtopWhiteboardDrill.tsx`, `SEWeek2PlatformOverview.tsx`) as ground truth — what boxes, colors, numbers, sequence the rep actually sees.
- Fix `buildStudyNote`: stop spreading `derived` over `base`. Keep `narrationInOneLine` (used only for the small reinforcement footer) but do NOT let `keyPoints` clobber curated content. Output a clear separation between curated study material and optional narration extract.
- Add per-week `WEEK_FALLBACK.whatsOnSlide` for the rare un-curated slide.

**2. `src/lib/fieldKitPdf.ts → renderSlidePagePortrait`**
- Rewrite the page body to the layout above:
  - Title block (unchanged)
  - "THE ONE-SENTENCE TAKEAWAY" → `inOneSentence` in a large 12pt bold ink line
  - Two-column row: "WHAT'S ON THE SLIDE" (new `whatsOnSlide` bullets) + "WHY A BUYER CARES" (`whyItMatters`)
  - "THE 3 IDEAS YOU MUST OWN" → numbered `keyIdeas` (curated, not parsed beats)
  - 2×2 grid: Key Terms · Defensible Facts · Watch-Out · How This Connects (data already present)
  - "CHECK YOURSELF" → 3 `checkYourself` questions as checkboxes
  - Optional small italic "From the coach narration:" line at the bottom with `narrationInOneLine` (single line, muted) — leaves a bridge to the audio without dominating the page
- Remove the current "KEY POINTS FROM THE NARRATION" numbered block — that is the section reps are complaining about.
- Tighten the page-budget math: every block has a fixed max height and an overflow rule that prefers cutting `facts` and `terms` to 3 each before truncating `keyIdeas` or `whatsOnSlide`.

**3. Cover page (`buildWeekFieldKitPdf`)**
- Update the "WHAT YOU'LL BE ABLE TO DO BY FRIDAY" sub-copy to point at the study-note format ("Every slide page is a self-contained study sheet: one-sentence takeaway, what's on the slide, three ideas, terms, facts, watch-out, and a self-check.") so the reader knows how to use the new layout.

**4. Out of scope**
- The on-screen Coach Card panel (`CoachCardPanel.tsx`) is unchanged — it stays a quick reference during delivery.
- Narration data (`salesEnablementNarration.ts`) and slide components are unchanged.
- Other PDF-style exports (`DeckPDFExportButton`, exec/customer-overview/tech decks) are unchanged.
- Glossary appendix and W3 Sell & Win appendix are unchanged.
